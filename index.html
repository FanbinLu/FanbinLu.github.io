About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Jiaya Jia and Prof. Chi-Wing Fu. I received my bachelor's degree in Automation from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2019. My expected graduation date is March 2027.

My research focuses on native multimodal models, computer-use (GUI) agents, and LLM code evolution. I study how models learn to generate visual content, interact with computer interfaces, and accumulate reusable algorithmic knowledge.

I am currently a research intern with Tencent Hunyuan's native multimodal model team, working on multimodal pretraining data and Code2Image agent pipelines for training data generation, HTML layout evaluation, and reinforcement learning. Previously, I worked on GUI agents and industrial anomaly detection at SmartMore.
